2. ADVENTURE FESTA: Character Scout

Task: Find 3 characters. For each one, decide whether it can become animation, not just a collectible.
Look for these design signals
  • Silhouette: recognizable from far away
  • Power system: what special ability could it have?
  • Weakness: what flaw makes it interesting?
  • World clue: what setting does it imply?
  • Motion: how would it move in animation?
